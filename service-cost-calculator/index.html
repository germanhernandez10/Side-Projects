How this is calculated
  • Automated conversations = volume × automation %, billed at the AI cost per conversation.
  • Non-automated conversations = volume × (1 − automation %); required handle minutes = that volume × AHT.
  • Productive minutes per agent per month = hours/week × 4.33 weeks/month × productive time %.
  • FTEs required = required handle minutes ÷ productive minutes per agent; headcount is rounded up to the next whole person.
  • Human labor cost = headcount × (base hourly rate × hours/week × 4.33 weeks) × (1 + gross margin %). Agents are paid for full scheduled hours regardless of the productivity assumption, which only affects how much handle-time capacity each agent supplies.
